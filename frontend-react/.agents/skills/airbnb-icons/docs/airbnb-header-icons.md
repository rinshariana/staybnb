# Airbnb header icon inventory

Explored on 2026-11-19 using the live public Airbnb site. Assets are saved in `frontend-react/public/icons/`. App components, CSS and asset references were not changed.

## Coverage

- Desktop expanded and compact header; All, Homes, Experiences and Services tabs.
- Main menu, language/region and currency panels, account-settings informational text.
- Destination suggestions, scrolling to the bottom, typed Paris results (location, city hall, neighborhood and amusement park artwork). Nearby location permission was not invoked.
- Dates and Flexible panels, past-date disabled state, previous/next arrows, date tolerance icons and selected flexible month artwork.
- Guests at zero and with an adult: disabled/enabled minus and plus controls; service-animal informational dialog inspected before closing. Its large illustration was excluded as non-icon imagery.
- Services dropdown: all nine category icons and selected Photography state.
- Account-entry popup inspected, including Google/Apple and Airbnb marks. No account information entered. Hosting redirected to an account gate; deeper hosting/account states remain unvisited.
- Mobile 626px header, search overlay, destination expansion/back control, search/date accordion and mobile bottom navigation. The price-colors information control appeared disabled; its explanation could not be opened. Mobile variants repeating desktop artwork were deduplicated where identical.
- Initial cookie notice text and controls observed; it disappeared before its preferences panel could be explored. No claim of coverage of that panel.

## Limits and exclusions

Four desktop navigation WebM exports failed in the browser asset exporter. Original static poster assets were saved instead; they are source images, not screenshots or converted frames. Mobile WebM variants were observed but not exported. CSS-drawn shapes (tab underlines, separators, pill/circle backgrounds, radio selection outlines) and font/text currency symbols were skipped. No icon-font assets were identified. Help, referral, co-host and gift-card destinations were inventoried as menu links; their full destination pages are outside header scope. No bookings, payments, messages, account changes or sign-in were performed.

## Verification

SVGs parsed successfully as XML and were checked for unresolved CSS variables, scripts, event handlers and external use dependencies. Image file signatures determine their extensions (Airbnb frequently serves WebP bytes through a PNG URL, including AVIF). All 68 saved files decoded successfully in the browser (40 SVG, 23 AVIF, 5 WebP). A local contact sheet was visually compared with the source. No application build/lint was needed for this asset-only task.
